Research

Lab-to-field domain adaptation

CORAL, CW-CORAL, DANN and CDAN for plant disease recognition when training images come from the lab and real leaves come from the field.

Model compression & quantization

Stage-wise attribution of accuracy loss in compression pipelines; INT8 post-training quantization; ~10× feature database compression without retrieval loss.

Edge deployment

Running diagnosis on Raspberry Pi-class hardware: latency budgets, hardware benchmarking infrastructure, deployable models under 3 MB.

Explainable AI

Grad-CAM and SHAP explanations for diagnosis models, aimed at decisions an agronomist can actually check.
